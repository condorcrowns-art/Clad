GLITCHTOPIA

Your home server is yours to build. The corrupted sectors are yours to purge.

Punch blocks to harvest data seeds. Plant them, grow them, and splice two seeds together to compile new tech. Every item does something — springs launch you, sentries fight for you, tesla coils chain-zap, pets follow and defend. Fish the ponds, dig for riches in THE MINESHAFT, climb THE STACK, trade with the wandering merchant, level up, and in each corrupted sector gather the cipher keys to unseal the boss gate — then purge all seven corrupted processes, from the FIREWALL DAEMON to KRAKEN.SYS to the invisible ROOTKIT to the SWARM QUEEN. Type any name in WORLDS [V] to travel there — every name is a world. Survive them all and the BOSS RUSH awaits.
